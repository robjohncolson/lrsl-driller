## Grading Criteria (E / P / I)

General guidance:
- Be fair and somewhat lenient on wording for explanations, but NOT lenient on math correctness.
- For numeric fields: the value must match exactly.
- For factoring fields: accept equivalent factorizations (order of factors, minor spacing).
- If you are not sure the student is correct, do NOT guess—grade lower (P or I).

### For "identityType" (choice: Sum of Cubes / Difference of Cubes):
- E: Matches expected.
- I: Wrong choice.

### For "aValue" and "bValue" (text: cube root values):
- E: Student's value matches the expected cube root (e.g., "5x" for 125x³).
  Accept equivalent forms: "5x" = "5·x" = "5*x".
- P: Student gave the other value (swapped a and b), OR gave the cube (e.g., "125x³" instead of "5x").
- I: Incorrect value.

### For "factoredForm" (text: complete factored expression):
- E (Essentially Correct):
  - Algebraically equivalent to the expected factorization.
  - Correct sign pattern (SOAP: Same, Opposite, Always Positive).
  - Answer is a product of factors, not the original expression.
  - Order of factors does not matter.
- P (Partially Correct):
  - Correct identity type and most structure, but one sign error OR one term wrong.
  - Example: right linear factor but wrong middle sign in trinomial.
- I (Incorrect):
  - Not equivalent, not factored, or wrong identity.

Key cube formulas:
- a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² − ab + b²)  [Sum: middle sign is MINUS]
- a³ − b³ = (a − b)(a² + ab + b²)  [Diff: middle sign is PLUS]

### For "identityChoice" (choice: Sum of Cubes / Difference of Cubes):
- E: Matches expected.
- I: Wrong choice.

### For "explanation" (textarea: why this identity):
- E:
  - States the expression uses addition (+) or subtraction (−), AND
  - Identifies that both terms are perfect cubes (mentions cube roots or shows a³ = term).
  - Does not need to be perfectly worded.
- P:
  - Mentions one of the two key elements (sign OR perfect cubes) but not both.
  - OR mentions both but explanation is very vague or incomplete.
- I:
  - Does not address why the identity was chosen, or reasoning is incorrect.

### For "termCoeff" (number: coefficient from binomial expansion):
- E: Exact match.
- P: Student got the Pascal coefficient OR the power of the constant correct, but not both multiplied together. OR correct magnitude but wrong sign.
- I: Not close / wrong method.

Common errors to watch for:
- Forgetting to multiply by the Pascal coefficient C(n,k)
- Getting the sign wrong ((-3)^odd is negative, (-3)^even is positive)
- Using the wrong row of Pascal's Triangle

### For "expandedPoly" (text: full polynomial in standard form):
- E:
  - All terms present with correct coefficients and correct signs.
  - Standard form (descending powers).
  - Accept minor formatting differences (spaces, explicit + signs, etc.).
- P:
  - Most terms correct (≥70%) but 1-2 sign errors or coefficient errors.
  - OR correct terms but not in standard form.
- I:
  - Fewer than 70% of terms correct, or fundamentally wrong approach.
