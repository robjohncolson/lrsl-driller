
**CARTRIDGE: U4-SIM**

**Topics:** 4.1 (Randomness), 4.2 (Simulation)  
**CED Skills:** 3.A (estimate via simulation), 4.B (interpret long-run meaning)

**PURPOSE:**  
Students practice writing correct simulation plans and interpreting simulation output. Matches the CED emphasis that simulations help students internalize probability before formal rules.

**ITEM MIX (10 items):**
- **5 "Simulation Plan" prompts** (short written) — design a simulation with all 5 components
- **3 "Digit Mapping" items** (MC or short answer) — assign digits to match a given probability
- **2 "Read Simulation Output" items** (MC) — interpret histogram/table from simulation results, use "at least"/"at most" language

**REQUIRED RESPONSE FORMAT (for Simulation Plan items):**
```
Random device:
Mapping:
Trial:
Repeat:
Estimate:
```

**GRADING RUBRIC (Simulation Plan):**
- **Full (E):** All 5 labels present AND mapping matches the probability AND trial definition matches the question AND repeat count stated (many/100+)
- **Partial (P):** Missing 1-2 labels but core idea is correct, OR mapping proportion is slightly off
- **Incorrect (I):** Not a simulation plan (no random mechanism / no mapping / no repetition)

**GRADING RUBRIC (Digit Mapping):**
- **Full (E):** Digit proportion exactly matches target probability (e.g., 8 digits for 80%)
- **Partial (P):** Close but off by one digit
- **Incorrect (I):** Proportion doesn't match (e.g., using 0-8 for 80% = 9 digits = 90%)

**KEY CONCEPTS TO TEST:**
- Random device must actually be random (coin, die, random digits, RNG)
- Mapping proportion must equal the probability (80% = 8 out of 10 digits)
- Trial = one complete run of the scenario
- Repeat = many trials (Law of Large Numbers — more trials → closer to true probability)
- "At most 2" means 0, 1, or 2 (sum those frequencies)
- "At least 3" means 3 or more (sum those frequencies)

**EXAMPLE PROMPTS:**

*Simulation Plan:*
> "Describe a simulation to estimate the probability of making exactly 4 free throws out of 5 attempts if you make 80% of your free throws."

*Expected:*
- Random device: random digits 0-9 (or 1-100)
- Mapping: 0-7 = make, 8-9 = miss (8 digits = 80%)
- Trial: 5 digits = one set of 5 free throws
- Repeat: 200+ trials
- Estimate: proportion of trials with exactly 4 makes

*Digit Mapping:*
> "To simulate a 15% chance of rain, which digits should represent 'rain'?"  
> A) 0-1  B) 0-14  C) 1-15  D) 0-9

*Answer:* A (if using 0-9) or B (if using 00-99). Key: 15% = 15 out of 100 OR approximately 1.5 out of 10.

*Read Output:*
> [Histogram of simulation results]  
> "Based on the simulation, what is the probability of at most 2 successes?"

*Answer:* Sum the relative frequencies for 0, 1, and 2.
