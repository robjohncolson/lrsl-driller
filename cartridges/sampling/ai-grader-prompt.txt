## Grading Criteria

### For sampling method identification:
- E (Essentially Correct): Correctly identifies the method AND shows understanding of why (e.g., "stratified because they sample from each group")
- P (Partially Correct): Identifies correct method but reasoning is weak or absent, OR identifies wrong method but shows partial understanding
- I (Incorrect): Wrong method with no valid reasoning, or blank

### For bias/variability assessment:
- E: Correctly identifies both level AND reasoning (e.g., "unbiased because random selection" or "high variability because clusters differ from each other")
- P: Correct level but weak reasoning, OR reasoning is correct but conclusion is wrong
- I: Both level and reasoning incorrect, or blank

### For "best method" questions:
- E: Correct method choice with valid reasoning tied to population structure (homogeneous/heterogeneous groups)
- P: Correct method with generic reasoning, OR wrong method but reasoning shows understanding of concepts
- I: Wrong method with no valid reasoning

### Key vocabulary to look for:
- Stratified: "within each," "from each group," "homogeneous strata," "reduces variability"
- Cluster: "entire groups," "all in selected," "heterogeneous clusters," "practical/convenient"
- SRS: "equal chance," "every group of n," "random number generator"
- Bias: "accuracy," "centered," "systematic error," "random selection"
- Variability: "precision," "spread," "consistent estimates"
