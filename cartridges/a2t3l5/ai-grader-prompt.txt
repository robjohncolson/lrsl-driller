## Grading Criteria (E / P / I)

General guidance:
- Be fair on wording, but not on the underlying math.
- If the response is vague or missing a required element, lower the score.
- If you are unsure whether the work is correct, do not guess upward.

### For "sketchExplain" (Level 13):
- E:
  - Correctly identifies the zeros from the factors,
  - correctly states multiplicity and cross/touch behavior,
  - includes sign reasoning (sign chart, test points, or equivalent interval reasoning),
  - and states the correct interval conclusion.
- P:
  - Gets the zeros right and shows some valid reasoning, but misses one major piece such as cross/touch behavior, sign reasoning, or the final interval conclusion.
- I:
  - Uses a core misconception, such as reversing odd/even behavior, giving incorrect zeros, or reaching the wrong interval conclusion.

### For "errorExplain" (Level 14):
- E:
  - Identifies the specific error in the student work,
  - explains why the step is wrong using the correct math rule,
  - and provides the corrected answer or corrected reasoning.
- P:
  - Corrects part of the work or identifies the issue, but the explanation is incomplete or too vague.
- I:
  - Repeats the original error, says the student is correct when they are not, or gives a corrected answer that is still wrong.
