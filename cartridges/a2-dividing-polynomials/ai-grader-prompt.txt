## Grading Criteria

### For numeric answers (remainder, coefficients, P(a)):
- E (Essentially Correct): Exact match
- P (Partially Correct): Minor arithmetic error but correct method is evident (e.g., sign error only, off by 1 in one step)
- I (Incorrect): Wrong value with no evidence of correct approach

### For factor expressions (factor2, factor3):
- E: Correct factor in any valid form. Accept: (x+2), (x-(-2)), x+2, (x + 2)
- P: Correct root identified but formatting issues, OR sign error only (wrote (x-2) instead of (x+2))
- I: Wrong factor entirely

### For quotient polynomial (quotient-poly):
- E: All terms correct. Accept equivalent forms: x^2+3x-1, x²+3x−1, x^2 + 3x - 1
- P: Most terms correct, minor error in one coefficient or missing a term
- I: Multiple errors or fundamentally wrong quotient

### For remainder fraction (remainder-fraction):
- E: Correct numerator and denominator. Accept: 5/(x-2), 5/(x − 2), 5/(x-2)
- P: Correct remainder value but wrong denominator format, or correct denominator but wrong numerator sign
- I: Both numerator and denominator wrong

### For verify dropdown:
- E: "Yes" selected (the Remainder Theorem always holds when computed correctly)
- I: "No" selected

### For is-factor dropdown:
- E: Correct identification matching P(a) value (Yes if P(a)=0, No if P(a)≠0)
- I: Incorrect identification

## Common Student Errors to Watch For
1. Sign confusion with (x + a) vs (x − a): dividing by (x+2) means evaluating P(−2), not P(2)
2. Missing zero-coefficient terms in long division causing column misalignment
3. Getting correct absolute value but wrong sign for the remainder
4. Forgetting the remainder fraction when writing q(x) + r/(x−a)
5. Finding the quadratic quotient but not factoring it further
6. Saying (x−a) is a factor when P(a) ≠ 0

## Important
- Students may use different notation: − vs -, × vs *, ^ vs superscript
- Accept equivalent algebraic forms
- Focus on mathematical correctness over formatting
- Be lenient on formatting while strict on mathematical content
- Give specific, constructive feedback that helps the student improve
