## Grading Criteria (E / P / I)

General guidance:
- Be fair and somewhat lenient on wording for explanations, but NOT lenient on math correctness.
- For numeric fields: the value must match the expected value.
- For factoring fields: accept equivalent factorizations (commutativity of factors, minor formatting differences).
- If you are not sure the student is correct, do NOT guess—grade lower (P or I).

### For "identityName" (multiple choice identity recognition):
- E: Student selection matches the expected identity name exactly (Difference of Squares / Square of a Sum / Difference of Cubes / Sum of Cubes).
- P: N/A (treat wrong as I).
- I: Any other choice.

### For "flashcardAnswer" (dropdown identity recall):
- E: Student selection matches the expected correct answer.
- P: N/A (treat wrong as I).
- I: Wrong option.

### For "rewriteChoice" (dropdown rewrite/expand/factor):
- E: Selected option matches the expected rewritten form.
- P: N/A (treat wrong as I).
- I: Wrong option.

### For "numericResult" (number):
- E: Numeric value matches expected.
- P: Very close (minor arithmetic slip) ONLY if clearly intended identity is correct.
- I: Not close / wrong method / non-numeric.

### For "factorAnswer" (text factorization):
- E (Essentially Correct):
  - The student’s expression is algebraically equivalent to the expected factorization, AND
  - The identity’s sign pattern is correct (especially the middle term sign in cube identities), AND
  - The answer is actually factored (a product), not just the original expression.
  - Order of factors does not matter.
- P (Partially Correct):
  - Correct identity type and most factor structure, but one sign error OR one term incorrect/missing.
  - Example: correct linear factor but quadratic factor has wrong middle sign.
- I (Incorrect):
  - Not equivalent, not factored, or shows a different identity than required.

Key things to check for cubes:
- a^3 − b^3 = (a − b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)
- a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 − ab + b^2)

### For "pascalValue" (number from Pascal’s Triangle / binomial coefficient):
- E: Correct value (missing entry, row sum 2^n, or binomial coefficient C(n,k)).
- P: Close but off by a small amount (e.g., one adjacent sum mistake).
- I: Incorrect.

### For "pascalExplain" (explanation of Pascal/Binomial reasoning):
- E:
  - Mentions a correct method such as:
    - “add the two numbers above / adjacent sums” (for missing entries), OR
    - “row sum is 2^n / doubles each row” (for row sums), OR
    - “binomial coefficient / n choose k / Row n of Pascal’s Triangle” (for coefficients),
  - and connects it to how they got the value.
- P:
  - Mentions Pascal’s Triangle or “choose” but explanation is vague or not connected to the value.
- I:
  - No relevant reasoning, or explanation is fundamentally incorrect.

### For "termCoeff", "termExpVar1", "termExpVar2" (numbers for a specific binomial term):
- E:
  - Each numeric value matches expected.
- P:
  - Off by a small amount due to a single arithmetic slip, OR
  - Exponents swapped but the student clearly describes the correct pattern in the explanation field (if present).
- I:
  - Incorrect and not close.

### For "errorExplain" (error analysis of a binomial term):
- E:
  - Identifies the key mistake (commonly: forgetting the binomial coefficient C(n,k) from Pascal’s Triangle),
  - AND states the correct reasoning (use C(n,k) and exponent pattern; exponents add to n).
- P:
  - Mentions Pascal/binomial coefficient OR exponent pattern, but misses the main mistake or is vague.
- I:
  - Does not address the mistake or gives incorrect reasoning.
