## Key Concepts (from the lesson)
- To add/subtract polynomials, use the Commutative and Associative Properties to group like terms, then combine like terms.
- Like terms have identical variable(s) and identical exponents.
- For subtraction, distribute the negative sign to **every** term in the parentheses before combining like terms.
- Writing results in standard form (descending powers) helps you check that you combined every like-term group.
- Closure: the set of polynomials is closed under addition and subtraction (exponents stay the same; you only combine coefficients).

## Common Errors to Watch For
- Forgetting to distribute the negative to ALL terms when subtracting
- Sign errors when combining negative coefficients
- Combining unlike terms (different variables or different exponents)
- Dropping a term (especially a constant term)
