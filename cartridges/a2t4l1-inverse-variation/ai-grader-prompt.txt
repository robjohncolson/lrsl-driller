## Grading Criteria

### For numeric answers (missingValue, applicationAnswer):
- E (Essentially Correct): Within stated tolerance of the expected value
- P (Partially Correct): Minor arithmetic error but correct method evident (e.g., found k correctly but divided wrong, or off by a small rounding amount)
- I (Incorrect): Wrong value with no evidence of correct approach

### For equation choice (equationChoice):
- E: Selected the correct inverse variation equation y = k/x
- P: Selected an equation with the correct |k| value but wrong sign
- I: Selected a direct variation or otherwise wrong equation

### For horizontal asymptote (horizontalAsymptote):
- E: Correctly identified the horizontal asymptote y = k from f(x) = a/(x-h) + k
- P: Gave the vertical asymptote instead of horizontal (common confusion)
- I: Wrong value entirely

### For translated equation (translatedEquation, translatedVariationEquation):
- E: Selected the correct translated form
- P: Correct vertical shift but wrong horizontal (or vice versa) — sign error in one component
- I: Both components wrong

## Key Vocabulary
- Inverse variation: xy = k (constant product)
- Constant of variation: the value k
- Reciprocal function: f(x) = 1/x
- Asymptote: a line the graph approaches but never reaches
- Translation: f(x) = a/(x-h) + k shifts h units horizontally, k units vertically

## Common Student Errors
1. Confusing direct variation (y = kx) with inverse variation (y = k/x)
2. Sign errors when k is negative (e.g., writing y = 12/x instead of y = -12/x)
3. Confusing horizontal and vertical asymptotes
4. Getting the sign of h wrong in translations: left 2 means (x+2) not (x-2)
5. Forgetting that the horizontal asymptote is y = k, not y = 0, after a vertical shift

## Important
- Accept equivalent numeric forms: 1.5, 3/2, 1.50
- Accept spacing variations: y=-4, y = -4, y= -4
- Focus on mathematical correctness over formatting
- Give specific, constructive feedback that helps the student improve
